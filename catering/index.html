Chai catering questions

Where do you cater?

Saskatoon and the surrounding area. We bring a mobile cart, so most indoor and outdoor venues work: halls, offices, backyards, parks and market grounds. We just need a bit of level ground and somewhere to set up.

Can we have hot and iced chai?

Yes. We pour hot masala chai, iced chai, or both: hot in winter, iced in summer, and whatever suits your crowd in between.

What drinks can we choose from?

Anything on our regular menu: the Classic Hot Chai, Iced Chai, Gulabo Rose Refresher, Iced Chocolate Chai, YXE's Mont Blanc, Iced Banana Bread Chai and Lavender Cold Foam Chai. Samosas can come too.

How much does chai catering cost?

It depends on how many guests, how long we pour and what's on the menu, so there are no fixed packages. Send us the details and we'll reply with a plan and a price.

Do you do weddings and cultural events?

Yes: receptions, mehndi mornings, sangeets, Diwali parties and late-night service. A chai cart at the reception does what a coffee urn never will.

Can we just order chai without the cart?

Yes. Hot chai by the cup and samosas by the dozen, picked up or delivered anywhere in Saskatoon, with 48 hours' notice. See bulk orders.
