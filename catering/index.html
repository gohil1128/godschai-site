What we cater

01 · Weddings

Weddings & receptions

A chai cart at the reception does what a coffee urn never will. Late-night service, mehndi mornings, or a station running all evening.

02 · Corporate

Office & corporate

Team mornings, client days, staff appreciation, conference breaks. We set up, pour, and clear out without leaving a mess behind.

03 · Private

Parties & markets

Birthdays, Diwali, house parties, community markets and school fundraisers. If people will be standing around talking, chai earns its place.

How it works

Tell us the basics. Date, rough headcount, where it is, and whether you want it hot or iced. Message us on Instagram — that's the fastest way to reach us.

We'll come back with a plan. How long we'll pour for, what we'll bring, and what it costs. No packages to decode.

We show up and pour. The cart brews on site, so guests get chai the way it's meant to be — fresh out of the pot, not out of a dispenser that's been sitting since breakfast.

What you can put on the menu

Anything from the regular menu works for events — the Classic Hot Chai, the Iced Chai, or the signature builds like Gulabo and YXE's Mont Blanc. Samosas pair well and travel well.

Where we go

Saskatoon and the surrounding area. We're a mobile cart, so most indoor and outdoor venues work — we just need a bit of level ground and somewhere to set up.
