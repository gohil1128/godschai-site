Know your cup

Chai tea latte vs masala chai

Sold as the same drink. Made two very different ways.

The short answer

The café version

Chai tea latte

Sweet chai syrup or powder topped with steamed milk. Nothing brewed. Sugar first, spice second, tea barely there.

The original

Masala chai

Whole spices and black tea simmered in water and milk until the pot turns caramel. Spice first, tea underneath, sugar optional.

Tell them apart in one sip

  • Sugar hits first? It’s syrup.
  • Only tastes of cinnamon? A powder blend. Real masala chai leads with cardamom.
  • Warmth in your throat? That’s ginger, properly simmered.
  • Got body? Chai boiled with milk is thicker than steamed milk over syrup.
