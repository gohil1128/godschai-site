Three tips

  • Chai ice cubes. Freeze a tray of chai. They melt into chai, not water.
  • Whole milk or barista oat. Both stay creamy cold. Almond tends to split.
  • Brew it a little too strong. Cold mutes flavour, so start bold. The base method is in our brew guide.
