{%- comment -%} 6. How did it turn out? Pure CSS: a chip shows its tip. {%- endcomment %}
How did your cup turn out?

That’s the one. Same cups, same shade next time, and tag us {{ site.social.instagram_handle }} if you pour it somewhere nice.

Give it one more rise, or go a shade darker. If it still tastes thin, the mix wants to be properly heaped, and whole milk carries the spice better than skim.

Pull it off a minute sooner, or pick a shade lighter. Past Extra kadak the tea turns bitter, so let the colour tell you when to stop.

It happens to everyone. Turn the heat down as it climbs and lift the pot off for a second; for 5 cups or more, use a pot twice the size of what’s in it.
