Brew it your way

Four steps. One proper cup.

Straight off the back of the bag. Stovetop only — this is how chai is supposed to be made.

Serves 2
01 Boil

1 cup water, 1 cup milk

Bring both to a boil together in a small pot. Equal parts, no measuring cup gymnastics.

02 Blend

2 tbsp God's Chai

Stir in the mix and sugar to taste. Give the bag a shake first — the spices settle.

03 Rise

Let it rise 2–3 times

Let it climb the pot, pull it back, repeat. This is where the magic happens — don't skip it.

04 Strain

Strain and enjoy

Pour through a fine strainer into your cup. Kadak, spiced, exactly right.
