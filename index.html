Brew Guide

BREW IT YOUR WAY

Follow our 5-step process — the same way we've made it from day one. Scan the QR on your pack to watch us brew it live on TikTok.

1
Add Water & Milk
Add 1 cup water + 1 cup milk to your pot.
2
Bring to a Boil
Heat on medium until it starts to boil.
3
Add God's Chai Mix
Add 2 tbsp of your God's Chai premix and stir well.
4
Let It Rise 2–3 Times
Allow the chai to rise and boil 2–3 times for maximum flavour.
5
Strain, Sweeten & Sip Slowly
Pour through a strainer, add your preferred sweetener, and take your time.
💡
Pro Tip
"Let it boil twice. That's the God's Chai way."
