Cup taste off?

  • Like hot milk: not enough tea, or the milk went in too early.
  • Bitter: the tea boiled too long.
  • Like a candle: too much cassia, or a clove too many.
  • Flat: it needs ginger or pepper.
  • Smells better than it tastes: the spice was pre-ground.
