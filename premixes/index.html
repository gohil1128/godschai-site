Why bother with ours

Most chai premixes on a Canadian shelf are sugar and powdered flavour with a bit of tea dust in it. You can taste the shortcut immediately — it's sweet, it's flat, and it doesn't smell like anything.

Ours is the recipe we've been pouring at pop-ups across Saskatoon: whole cardamom, ginger, cassia, clove and pepper from Kerala, ground in small batches, with single-origin Assam that's malty enough to stand up to milk. Nothing added to make it cheaper.
